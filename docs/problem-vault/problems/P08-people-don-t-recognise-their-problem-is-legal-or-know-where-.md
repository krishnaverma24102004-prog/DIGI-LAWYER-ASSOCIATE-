# P08. People don't recognise their problem is legal or know where to go, and legal aid reaches only a sliver of those eligible

**Severity:** critical · **Size:** big · **Region:** Global and India

**Who has it:** Low-income and rural Indians, first-generation litigants, migrant workers; globally 1.4 billion people with unmet justice needs; DLSAs and legal aid counsel

## What goes wrong

Most people with a tenancy, wage, consumer, domestic violence or cheque problem never treat it as legal, never approach a lawyer, and never use free legal aid they qualify for. Over 80% of India's population is eligible, but only about 1.5 crore people received legal aid from 1995 to 2019. The system is thinly staffed and legal aid counsel are underpaid and paid late, so eligible people pay private lawyers they can't afford or give up, often after the limitation period has run.

## Evidence of scale

- WJP: 1.4 billion people have unmet civil and administrative justice needs; in its 101-country survey only 29% of people with a legal problem sought advice, only 29% saw the problem as legal, 16% found it hard to find the money and 17% gave up. ([source](https://worldjusticeproject.org/news/groundbreaking-study-reveals-unmet-legal-needs-worldwide))
- India Justice Report 2019: over 80% of about 1.25 billion people are eligible for legal aid, but only about 15 million received it since 1995; only five states used over 90% of NALSA funds (2017-18). ([source](https://www.barandbench.com/columns/india-justice-report-2019-15-million-out-of-1-billion-eligible-indians-provided-legal-aid-services))
- One legal aid lawyer per 18,609 people (CHRI 2018); an Agra retainer earns Rs 5,000 against the Rs 15,000 guideline; 34% of legal aid counsel cited delayed payment; private fees start around Rs 20,000 against Rs 18,000 per case paid to legal aid counsel in Delhi; nearly 60% of cases take over a year at subordinate level. ([source](https://idronline.org/article/livelihoods/why-is-the-quality-of-indias-free-legal-aid-so-poor/))
- LSC Justice Gap 2022: low-income Americans got no or inadequate help for 92% of substantial civil legal problems; the most common reason for not seeking help was believing they couldn't afford it. ([source](https://lsc.gov/press-release/low-income-americans-face-immense-justice-gap-according-new-legal-services-corporation-report))
- UNDP India names the digital divide and the legal literacy gap as primary mechanics of exclusion; 24% of respondents from marginalised groups never attended school. ([source](https://www.policyedge.in/p/undp-enhancing-meaningful-access-to-justice-in-india))

## Why current tools don't solve it

Harvey, CoCounsel, Lexis+ AI and Clio sell to lawyers who already have the client. Government channels (Tele-Law, NALSA helplines) rely on thin human staff. No tool offers vernacular, voice-first, PII-safe triage that tells a person 'this is a legal problem, this is the statute and deadline, you may qualify for legal aid, here is a lawyer' and hands a structured intake to a firm or DLSA, while staying informational and not advice.

## Panel verdict

**Status:** open (round -)

**Effort:** L

### What software can fix

Software can do three things. (1) Help an adult see that their problem may be legal, in their own language, on channels they already use: WhatsApp, a phone call or the keypad. (2) For orgs verified as legal-aid bodies, or firms with a recorded pro bono commitment, work out in code whether the person is likely eligible for free legal aid under s.12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987. (3) Point everyone to the right DLSA/TLSC and the NALSA 15100 helpline, and hand a consented, PII-masked intake packet to the accountable org. On every channel, nothing a person says is transcribed, stored in readable form or shown to staff until they have consented and confirmed they are an adult. That gate runs before every other branch, and a DB trigger backs it up. A self-declared minor at the gate gets a fixed card pointing to free help, and their data is hard-deleted, leaving only a per-day count. If an adult conversation later contains a present-tense age statement, which the patterns only match when it is anchored to an age unit or the end of a clause, the system first decides in code whether the conversation is sensitive. A conversation is sensitive if it was handed off or viewed by staff, or if any of its text matches the abuse lexicon. Sensitive held text is written to Postgres at once, encrypted, under legal_hold and readable only by supervisors, and it never expires. Only unseen, non-sensitive text is held in Redis, and every held message is re-checked against the abuse lexicon as it arrives, so a disclosure is persisted whether or not the age question is ever answered. Deletion happens only after a confirmed 'under 18 now' answer on a conversation with nothing sensitive in it.

### What it can't fix

Software can't hire or pay legal aid counsel (1 per 18,609 people, Rs 5,000 retainers, late payment). It can't fix connectivity or reach people with no phone. It can't make DLSAs answer quickly, and it can't decide eligibility: the DLSA decides and we only pre-screen. It can't stop limitation running. It can't verify anyone's age or a guardian's identity over WhatsApp or a phone call. We rely on self-declaration at the gate, then a confirmed answer to a fixed question. A minor who says they are an adult and never makes an anchored present-tense age statement won't be caught. The abuse lexicon is a fixed word list. A disclosure in words it doesn't contain, in a non-sensitive unseen conversation, in a conversation where the person never answers the age question, can still expire from Redis after 24 h. We say so plainly and do not claim to detect every disclosure. Supervisors can grow the lexicon through the approval flow. While a held message waits for a supervisor, the handling lawyer sees only a placeholder. A supervisor can release it in one audited step, but software can't guarantee that a supervisor is on duty at 2 a.m. The age question carries 1098 and 112 for that reason. It can't make the s.19 POCSO reporting decision: a named supervisor makes and records it. It can't control what Meta and Twilio keep, and it can't recall text already sent to Anthropic or audio already sent to Deepgram before a later under-18 confirmation. It will not be a lawyer marketplace or ranking (BCI Rules 36/37). Languages that Deepgram STT or Tesseract can't handle get only the keypad menu and pre-recorded cards.

### What the user sees

Caller and WhatsApp gate: unchanged from round 5. Consent notice ('Reply YES to continue', with no LAWYER offer), then 'Are you 18 or older? 1 yes, 2 no'. At the gate, any other input re-sends the gate question. Answering 2 gives the minor card (DLSA, 15100, 1098, 112). Adults get the numbered menu. Late age statement, new behaviour: 'मैं 16 साल की हूँ' or 'I am 16 years old' gets the fixed question 'आपकी उम्र अभी 18 से कम है? 1 हाँ, 2 नहीं। अगर कोई आपको नुकसान पहुँचा रहा है तो 1098 या 112 पर कॉल करें।' and the AI says nothing else. 'I am 15 minutes away from court', 'I'm 16th in the queue' and 'मेरी उम्र 17 थी जब शादी हुई' change nothing. Handling lawyer (/inbox) on a handed-off conversation that triggers the detector: the conversation stays in their list with the banner 'Age statement detected: new messages held for supervisor review (supervisor alerted 14:02)'. Each held message appears as a dated placeholder row 'Message held for supervisor review', with no text. When a supervisor releases a message, its text appears with 'Released by <supervisor> at <time>'. Lawyer (/inbox) on an AI-intake conversation in age_check: the badge 'Age check pending: AI paused', with no held text shown. Supervisor (/triage, owner, partner and legal_aid_supervisor only): a 'Held for POCSO assessment' queue with three sections. 'Abuse disclosure held' is pinned top and red. 'Age check: sensitive, awaiting answer' and 'Age check unanswered > 24 h' follow. Each item shows the decrypted held messages read-only, in order, with the lexicon term that matched highlighted, and the buttons 'Release to handling lawyer' (when the person answered 2, or after the supervisor has reviewed it) and 'Record s.19 assessment' (report made with reference, or no report required with reasons). Associates and paralegals see neither the queue nor any held text. The 'Minors redirected' tile shows counts per day, channel and language, as before.

### What the system does

1-6. Unchanged from round 5. Triage mode flags. The phone gate runs before STT (media frames discarded undecoded, pre-rendered hash-checked prompts, caller_hash only). _gate_turn() is the first branch in _handle for triage numbers, with a closed vocabulary, and LAWYER, intake_enabled and free text can't leave the gate. The whatsapp_gate_guard trigger. Pre-gate minimisation: HMAC wa_id, raw id and name only in Redis wa_gate (24 h), the RLS policy excludes 'gate', voice notes are withheld, and a 24 h gate purge runs. msg_ref() HMAC message IDs for inbound and outbound, with dedup by hash and 30-day previous-key overlap. triage_log() logs only msg_ref, status codes and exception class names, with exc_info off. Gate minor_stop hard-deletes and writes a minor_block plus an identifier-free count and audit row. 7. Late age statements, rewritten. (a) Anchored detector. data/legal_aid/minor_patterns.json holds compiled, anchored regexes, run after NFC normalisation and conversion of Devanagari, Tamil and Gujarati digits to ASCII. English: `\bI(?:'m| am)\s+(1[0-7])\s*(?:years?|yrs?|y\.?o\.?)\b(?:\s+old)?` OR `\bI(?:'m| am)\s+(1[0-7])(?:\s+(?:now|only))?\s*(?:[.!?,;।]|$)`. The number must be followed by an age unit, or by an optional 'now'/'only' and then the end of a clause. Hindi: `मैं\s+(1[0-7])\s*(?:साल|वर्ष|बरस)\s+(?:का|की|के)\s+(?:हूँ|हूं)`, `मेरी\s+उम्र\s+(?:अभी\s+)?(1[0-7])\s*(?:साल|वर्ष)?\s*(?:है|हैं)\b`, and `मैं\s+(?:कक्षा|class)\s+[5-9]\s+में\s+पढ़(?:ता|ती)\s+(?:हूँ|हूं)`. Marathi uses `वय\s+(1[0-7])\s+(?:वर्षे|वर्ष)\s+आहे` and `मी\s+(1[0-7])\s+वर्षांचा|वर्षांची\s+आहे`. Tamil uses `எனக்கு\s+(1[0-7])\s+வயது`. Hinglish uses `\bmai(?:n)?\s+(1[0-7])\s*(?:saal|sal|years?)\s+(?:ka|ki|ke)\s+(?:hu|hoon|hun)\b`. After that, the per-sentence past-marker exclusion from round 5 runs (था/थी/थे/थीं, was/were, 'when I was', 'at the age of', जब मैं, उस समय, की उम्र में, होते, वयात). The detector runs only in status 'intake' or 'lawyer' and is off in guardian sessions. (b) Classify at the moment of detection, in code, inside the conversation lock: sensitive = (status == 'lawyer') OR staff_viewed_at IS NOT NULL OR a handoff packet exists OR abuse_match(any stored message body in this conversation, or the triggering text). abuse_match uses data/legal_aid/abuse_lexicon.json after normalisation: यौन, छेड़छाड़, बलात्कार, गलत तरीके से छुआ, गंदी हरकत, 'touched me', 'sexual', 'rape', 'abuse', 'POCSO', plus Marathi and Tamil terms, as before. (c) Sensitive path: status 'age_check_sensitive'. legal_hold = true is set on the conversation and on its transcripts row at once. The triggering message, and every later inbound message while the status is held, is written immediately to the new table whatsapp_held_messages (id, conversation_id, seq, received_at, kind, body_enc, nonce, key_kid, lexicon_hits text[], released_at, released_by). body_enc is AES-256-GCM with the org's hold key (TRIAGE_HOLD_KEYS, versioned by kid, envelope-wrapped). whatsapp_messages gets a placeholder row '[held: supervisor review]' pointing at the held id, so the handling lawyer sees that a message arrived and when. There is no TTL and no Redis copy. Held rows are decrypted only in GET /api/v1/triage/held/{conversation_id}, after a role check for owner, partner or legal_aid_supervisor. RLS on whatsapp_held_messages allows select to those roles only, and the API never returns body_enc. Supervisors get an in-app alert, marked urgent when lexicon_hits is non-empty. The fixed, hash-checked age question (with 1098/112) is sent. If the status was 'lawyer', the AI stays off as before. Lawyer-initiated replies still work, so the lawyer is not muted. (d) Non-sensitive path (status 'intake', never viewed, no packet, no abuse match): status 'age_check'. The AI is paused, the triggering text and later inbound text go to Redis age_hold:{id} (TTL 24 h) and are stored as '[held: age check]', and the age question is sent. Every held message runs through abuse_match when it arrives, before it goes to Redis. On a hit, escalate() runs in the lock: it writes every Redis-held text in seq order, plus the new one, to whatsapp_held_messages, deletes the Redis key, sets status 'age_check_sensitive' and legal_hold, and alerts supervisors urgently. Nothing waits for the answer. (e) Answers. '2' on the non-sensitive path restores the previous status, writes the Redis texts to whatsapp_messages in order, deletes the key, resumes, and audits 'triage.age_check_cleared', as in round 5. '2' on the sensitive path restores the previous status. If no held row has lexicon_hits, the held rows are released into whatsapp_messages (decrypted server-side and written as normal messages), legal_hold is cleared, and 'triage.age_check_cleared' is audited. If any lexicon hit exists, the rows are released to the handling lawyer's view, but legal_hold stays and the item stays in the supervisor queue as 'Abuse disclosure: adult declared' until a supervisor closes it with a recorded review. '1' on the non-sensitive path calls purge_minor() (hard-delete the conversation, messages, transcript and Redis keys; daily count; identifier-free audit row), as before. '1' on the sensitive path calls freeze_for_pocso(): status 'frozen_pocso', the held rows kept, legal_hold kept, the minor card sent, and the item kept in the queue until a pocso_assessments row exists. 'no_report_required' then purges, including whatsapp_held_messages. 'report_made' retains everything. (f) No answer. Non-sensitive 'age_check' after 24 h: the Redis text (which by construction never matched the lexicon) expires, the conversation stays paused, and it appears in the supervisor queue as 'Age check unanswered' with counts only. 'age_check_sensitive' after 24 h: nothing expires. The worker's 'task_age_check_escalate' cron moves it to 'Age check unanswered > 24 h' in the supervisor queue and re-alerts. Every delete path refuses it: purge_minor, the gate cron, the migration 013 trash, the 30-day adult purge, and the BEFORE DELETE trigger, which refuses rows with legal_hold or a status in ('age_check_sensitive', 'frozen_pocso') on whatsapp_conversations, whatsapp_messages, whatsapp_held_messages and transcripts. (g) Release to the handling lawyer: POST /api/v1/triage/held/{id}/release (supervisor roles, audited 'triage.held_released' with supervisor id and held ids, no text). It sets released_at/by, and the lawyer's inbox then shows the text. (h) Trigger whatsapp_gate_guard is extended. Allowed transitions are intake|lawyer -> age_check|age_check_sensitive, age_check -> age_check_sensitive|intake|(delete via purge_minor), and age_check_sensitive -> frozen_pocso|previous status. Leaving age_check_sensitive needs a matching audit row id passed via a session setting (set_config('triage.audit_id')), so a raw UPDATE can't clear it. (i) Phone mirrors this. On a detector match, segments are held in memory and the fixed audio question (with 1098/112) is played. Each held segment runs abuse_match as it arrives. On a hit, the call transcript is persisted at once as a transcripts row with legal_hold and status age_check_sensitive, held segments go to whatsapp_held_messages' phone twin call_held_segments (same encryption and RLS), and supervisors are alerted. If the call ends with no answer, a lexicon hit stays persisted. Without one, the held segments are dropped and the call is not persisted. DTMF '1' or '2' follows (e). 8-15. Unchanged: the guardian path with fresh origin='guardian', the screening gate needs legal_aid_verified_at or an audited pro bono commitment, compose_card's hash-checked block order, eligibility in code from s12_categories.json and income_ceilings.json, the menu state machine, the DLSA directory, recipient-specific handoff consent with build_packet, and PiiMasker before every model call.

### Why current tools fail and this won't

Harvey, CoCounsel, Lexis+ AI and Clio start once the lawyer already has the client. Tele-Law and the NALSA helpline depend on scarce staff and give every caller the same menu. WhatsApp bots keep numbers and message IDs from the first word, put 'talk to a human' ahead of any consent or age check, and show every chat to every staff member. Child-safety filters either do nothing or delete on a keyword. That would destroy an adult survivor's account of being married at 16, or a lawyer's 'I am 15 minutes away' message. In ours, the gate is a state machine backed by a trigger. Age patterns are anchored and past-tense aware. A match only pauses and asks. Whether held text is preserved is decided in code before anything can expire, and it is re-checked on every message. Abuse disclosures and handed-off conversations go straight to encrypted, supervisor-only, undeletable storage, without waiting for a frightened child to answer. Deletion is reserved for a confirmed child whose conversation nobody saw and which discloses nothing.

### Safeguards

DPDP s.6/s.9: nothing is processed beyond what the gate needs until consent and self-declared adult status, with the gate first in code and enforced by a trigger. Before the gate, the number and all Meta message IDs are keyed HMACs, and logs carry only msg_refs, status codes and exception class names. POCSO s.19/s.21: whether held text is preserved is decided deterministically at detection time and again on every message. Abuse-lexicon hits, handed-off conversations and staff-viewed conversations go straight to AES-GCM-encrypted, supervisor-only storage under legal_hold, with no TTL. Every delete path refuses it, triggers included, until a named supervisor records the s.19 assessment. The software never makes the reporting decision. Text from a client to their own lawyer is never destroyed by a TTL. At worst it is held for a supervisor, who releases it in one audited step. Redis-only holding is limited to unseen, non-sensitive text, and every message is re-checked before it is held there. Patterns are anchored and past-tense aware, so ordinary adult sentences don't trigger them. The age question carries 1098 and 112. Phone audio isn't decoded before the keypad gate. PII is masked before any model call. BCI Rules 36/37: no ranking, the free DLSA route comes first, and the fee notice is locked. UPL: legal text comes only from hash-checked cards. Approval points: an owner, partner or supervisor approves cards, notices, the lexicons and the pattern files. Supervisors release held text and make the s.19 assessment. The platform admin verifies legal-aid orgs.

### How the panel's objections were answered

(1) A disclosure no longer expires while waiting for an answer. Every message held under age_check runs through abuse_match as it arrives, before it goes to Redis. On a hit, escalate() immediately writes all held texts in order to whatsapp_held_messages (encrypted, supervisor-only RLS), deletes the Redis copy, sets age_check_sensitive and legal_hold, and alerts supervisors, without waiting for '1'. The triggering text and all stored messages are also checked at the moment of detection. The test you asked for is the first acceptance test: age statement, then abuse message, then 25 h with no reply, gives a row readable by a supervisor JWT and by no associate JWT. (2) (i) The patterns are anchored. The number must be followed by an age unit (years/yrs/y.o./साल/वर्ष/बरस/वय/வயது/saal), or by optional 'now'/'only' and then the end of a clause. Negative fixtures cover 'I am 15 minutes away from court', 'I'm 16th in the queue', 'I am 17 km', 'I am 16 hours late' and 'मैं 15 मिनट में', and each must leave a lawyer-status conversation untouched. (ii) The abuse lexicon runs on each held message as it arrives, and a hit freezes and persists it whether or not '1' is answered. (iii) Conversations in status 'lawyer', or with staff_viewed_at set or a handoff packet, never use Redis or a TTL. Held messages are written at once, AES-256-GCM encrypted, to whatsapp_held_messages with supervisor-only RLS. With no answer within 24 h, task_age_check_escalate moves the conversation to the supervisor queue and nothing expires. (iv) Tests cover no-answer with abuse (preserved), the lawyer-status false positive (nothing held, nothing lost) and a lawyer-status true positive with no abuse (persisted, released by a supervisor, nothing lost). (3) Sensitivity is checked at detection time, in the lock. A conversation that was already viewed, handed off or in 'lawyer' status, or whose held text matches the lexicon, goes to 'age_check_sensitive', with immediate persistence under legal_hold, supervisor-only access and an alert, and never expires. Redis is used only for unseen, non-sensitive conversations. To avoid blinding the lawyer, they see a timestamped placeholder for every held message, a banner naming the alerted supervisor, and the text itself once a supervisor releases it in one audited action. The lawyer can still reply. Your test is the second acceptance test: a lawyer-status conversation receives a present-tense age message that matches the lexicon, there is no reply for 25 h, and the text still exists under legal_hold and is visible to the supervisor.

### Still blocking

- The two age questions use opposite key meanings, and the late-question answer triggers an irreversible hard delete on a single keypress. At the gate the person was trained that '1' means 'yes, I am 18 or older' and '2' means minor. The late question is 'आपकी उम्र अभी 18 से कम है? 1 हाँ, 2 नहीं' ('Are you under 18 now? 1 yes, 2 no'), and per (e) '1' runs purge_minor() on the non-sensitive path, or freeze_for_pocso() on the sensitive path, while '2' clears. An adult, often low-literacy and on the keypad or IVR, who presses '1' out of the habit the gate just taught them loses their whole intake conversation and transcript to a hard delete with no recovery. A child who presses '2' because '2 = minor' at the gate is cleared, and the AI resumes talking to them. Under DTMF this is even more likely. Fix I would accept: (a) phrase the late question with the same polarity as the gate ('Are you 18 or older now? 1 yes, 2 no'), so '2' always means under 18 in every prompt and channel; (b) before purge_minor, send a fixed, hash-checked confirmation ('You said you are under 18. Your messages will be deleted and you will get free-help numbers. Reply 2 again to confirm, 1 if you are 18 or older'); (c) add tests asserting the same key mapping across the gate and late cards in hi/en/mr/ta and on DTMF, and that a single answer never purges.
- The stated Marathi detector is wrong as written, and it breaks the core promise that ordinary adult sentences don't trigger a hold. In `मी\s+(1[0-7])\s+वर्षांचा|वर्षांची\s+आहे`, alternation has the lowest precedence. The second branch therefore matches 'वर्षांची आहे' anywhere, with no age number: 'आई 60 वर्षांची आहे' ('my mother is 60 years old'), or 'ही इमारत 40 वर्षांची आहे' ('this building is 40 years old'). The first branch has no present-tense anchor, and the past-marker list lacks masculine 'होतो' and 'होती'. So 'मी 16 वर्षांचा होतो तेव्हा' ('when I was 16') matches. In a 'lawyer' conversation each of these moves the client's messages to their own lawyer into supervisor-only storage and withholds them from the handling lawyer. That diverts confidential client communications and risks missed deadlines on a false positive. The 'false positive does nothing' test has no Marathi negatives, so this would ship. Fix I would accept: group the alternation as `मी\s+(1[0-7])\s+वर्षांच(?:ा|ी)\s+आहे`, require a captured 1[0-7] in every pattern (enforced at load by the pattern-file validator), and add होतो/होती/होते to past_markers. Add Marathi negative fixtures ('आई 60 वर्षांची आहे', 'मी 16 वर्षांचा होतो तेव्हा') to the lawyer-status false-positive test, and add a CI check that every pattern in minor_patterns.json has exactly one age capture group.
- The detector patterns as written fail the proposal's own acceptance tests and miss real disclosures. (1) The Hindi pattern `मेरी\s+उम्र\s+(?:अभी\s+)?(1[0-7])\s*(?:साल|वर्ष)?\s*(?:है|हैं)\b` ends in `\b`. In Python's re, the matra 'ै' (U+0948) and the anusvara are not word characters, so there is no boundary after 'है'. I ran it: 'मेरी उम्र अभी 17 है', 'मेरी उम्र 16 साल है।' and 'मेरी उम्र 16 है और' all return no match. The positive test 'मेरी उम्र अभी १७ है' therefore fails, and a child stating their age in the most common Hindi form is never asked the age question. (2) In the Marathi pattern `मी\s+(1[0-7])\s+वर्षांचा|वर्षांची\s+आहे`, the alternation is ungrouped. The second branch matches any 'वर्षांची आहे' with no age at all: 'ही इमारत 50 वर्षांची आहे' (the building is 50 years old) matches, and the capture group is None. That pauses the AI on adult conversations and can hit a None-handling crash. Fix I would accept: replace the trailing `\b` after Indic text with a lookahead `(?=[\s।.!?,;]|$)`, or use the `regex` module with a documented word-boundary mode. Group the Marathi alternation as `मी\s+(1[0-7])\s+(?:वर्षांचा|वर्षांची)\s+आहे`. Add a CI test that loads data/legal_aid/minor_patterns.json as shipped and runs every positive and negative fixture through the compiled patterns, with each fixture asserting a matched age in 10-17, including Marathi and Tamil negatives about non-person subjects.
